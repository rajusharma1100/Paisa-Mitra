Questions

Short answers.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. There is no account and no developer server. See the Privacy Policy.

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free and supported by Google AdMob ads.

How do I back up my data?

Settings, then Backup. It saves a file to a place you choose, and Restore brings everything back.

I forgot my PIN.

Tap "Forgot PIN" and verify with your phone's screen lock to reset it.
