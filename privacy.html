Privacy Policy

Paisa Mitra · Last updated: 17 November 2026

Paisa Mitra ("the app", "we") is an expense tracker and small-business ledger (Khata, Cashbook, Billing, Inventory) for Android. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled. Package name: com.aistudio.paisamitra.pmsvqy

1. Your financial data stays on your device

All Khata ledgers, Cashbook entries, expense and income transactions, budgets, inventory items, invoices, recurring rules, savings goals and bill reminders are stored only on your phone. Paisa Mitra has no developer servers and no account or login. We never upload, receive, sell or share your financial records.

2. App lock and security

If you turn on App Lock, your PIN is stored only as a salted cryptographic hash (PBKDF2) and never as plain text. Fingerprint or screen-lock checks are done by the official Android system prompts. We never receive your PIN, fingerprint or face data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Paisa Mitra shows ads through Google AdMob. When ads are shown, Google may collect and process your Android Advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

  • In regions where consent is required (for example the EEA and UK), the app shows a Google consent form (UMP) and you can change your choice later in the app under Ad Consent & Privacy Options.
  • You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization anytime in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • We do not send your financial entries, parties, invoices or contacts to AdMob.

4. Permissions

  • Contacts (optional): asked only when you tap "Pick from Contacts" in Khata to fill a party's name and phone number. Contact data is used on your device and is not uploaded.
  • Notifications (optional): to show bill reminders, daily reminders and budget alerts, if you turn them on.
  • Biometric / screen lock (optional): only to unlock the app on your device.
  • Internet and network state: used to load ads and the consent form.

5. Photos, backups and exports

Photos you attach to Khata entries are chosen through the Android photo picker and are kept on your device. Backups, PDF, CSV and Excel exports are saved only to a location that you choose. We do not have access to them. Please keep your backup files safe because they contain your financial data.

6. Actions you start (WhatsApp, SMS, email, UPI)

When you tap actions such as sending a payment reminder on WhatsApp or SMS, sharing an invoice, showing a UPI QR code, or sending feedback by email, the app opens that other app and passes only the text you chose to send. Those apps are governed by their own privacy policies. The UPI QR code is generated on your device from the UPI ID you enter.

7. Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, financial records or usage analytics. The app does not use Firebase, analytics or crash-reporting services of its own.

8. Children

Paisa Mitra is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data deletion

Because your data is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Paisa Mitra → Storage. Backup or export files that you saved must be deleted by you.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will change when we do.
